TARSH CREATIVE, WEDDING PAGE MEDIA
==================================

Drop your files straight into this folder using the exact names below.
Anything you have not added yet shows a soft placeholder on the page,
so the site never looks broken while you are still choosing clips.

THE STRIP AT THE TOP OF THE PAGE
Five portrait clips that walk through a wedding day.

  hero1.mp4   details and getting ready
  hero2.mp4   an emotional ceremony moment
  hero3.mp4   the couple
  hero4.mp4   champagne or celebrating
  hero5.mp4   the dance floor

THE STORY SECTIONS

  story_morning.mp4     getting ready
  story_ceremony.mp4    the ceremony, this one runs full width so
                        landscape or a wide crop works best here
  story_party.mp4       the party

MEET TARSH

  tarsh.jpg    a photo of you working at a wedding, portrait

RECENT WORK GRID
Six portrait clips. These all link through to your Instagram.

  work1.mp4  work2.mp4  work3.mp4
  work4.mp4  work5.mp4  work6.mp4

WHAT TO EXPORT

  Video    mp4, H.264, portrait 1080 x 1920, 6 to 12 seconds,
           no sound needed, aim for under 4 MB each
  Photos   jpg, under about 1 MB

OPTIONAL BUT NICE
For any clip you can also add a still frame with the same name and a
.jpg ending, for example hero1.jpg. It shows instantly while the video
loads, which makes the page feel faster on a phone.

WANT TO CHANGE THE WORDS OR THE ORDER
Everything on the page, all the copy and the list of files above, lives
in one file: app/lib/wedding.ts

ONE THING TO KNOW
The website is built ahead of time, so a new clip appears once the site
rebuilds. If you are adding files through GitHub that happens by itself
within a minute or two of saving. If Adam is doing it locally he will
push it up for you.
